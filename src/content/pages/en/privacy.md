Last updated: November 4, 2026

全球動力科技股份有限公司 (Big x Reality; “the Company”, “we”) operates the official Big x Reality website (“this website”). In accordance with Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act (“PDPA”) and related regulations, this Policy explains how this website collects, processes and uses personal data. This Policy applies only to this website; other websites reached through links on this website are governed by their own privacy policies.

## 1. Website browsing and preferences

This is a static website without member accounts. It currently loads neither Google Analytics nor advertising tracking scripts. The website uses browser localStorage to remember the animation pause preference (bxr-motion) across pages. This setting contains no inquiry form data and is not transmitted as an advertising tracking identifier. It can be removed by resuming animations or clearing website data in the browser. Sections 2 and 3 describe inquiry form and email data processing; Section 5 describes hosting access logs.

## 2. Online inquiry form

The product, solution and “Contact BXR” pages provide an online inquiry form. At the bottom of the form are two buttons, “Agree and submit” and “Do not agree”. Selecting “Agree and submit” means you agree to this Policy, and only then is the form sent. If you select “Do not agree”, or until you submit, nothing you have entered is sent to us or stored in your browser.

**Data collected**

- Data you enter: name, company / organization and email address (required); job title, country / region and phone number (optional); inquiry topic, selected products and solutions (multiple selections), and your requirements.
- Recorded on submission: time of submission, the page path and language from which the form was sent, a system-generated inquiry reference, and the version of this Policy you agreed to. Our receiving script does not record your IP address.

**How it is processed**: Your browser sends the form directly to a receiving script that we built with Google Apps Script, and the data is stored in our Google Sheets spreadsheet, accessible only to authorized Company personnel. Google's cloud services are provided by Google LLC (United States), and the data may be stored in any country or region where Google operates data centers. On submission your browser connects to Google's servers (script.google.com and script.googleusercontent.com), and Google may log connection information under its own privacy policy. If email notifications for new inquiries are enabled, the inquiry content is also sent to the Company’s designated mailbox.

**Purpose**: To respond to your inquiry, provide product demonstrations and information, discuss cooperation, and provide follow-up customer service (specific purpose: 090 consumer and customer management and services).

**Retention period**: Data is retained for the period necessary to respond to the inquiry, provide follow-up services and meet legal obligations. When the purpose ends or the retention period expires, data is deleted, its use is stopped or it is de-identified as required by applicable law.

**Region of use**: Taiwan (R.O.C.) and the regions where Google's cloud services process data.

**Who uses the data and how**: Our sales and customer service staff reply and contact you by email, phone or similar means. We do not provide your data to third parties except as required by law or with your consent.

If a required field is left blank, or if you do not agree to this Policy, the form is not sent. You can still contact us by email as described in Section 3.

## 3. Contacting us by email

The “Contact BXR” page and the footer provide an email link (service@gptt.com.tw). Selecting it opens the email application on your device with a subject line and prompts for the following: name, company / organization, job title, country, phone number and requirements. Whether to send the email and what to include is up to you; this website itself does not receive or store this content.

When you send an email, we receive:

- Your email address and any information you include in the message (such as name, company / organization, job title, country, phone number and requirements).
- Header information added automatically by your email service.

**Purpose**: To respond to your inquiry, provide product demonstrations and information, discuss cooperation, and provide follow-up customer service (specific purpose: 090 consumer and customer management and services).

**Retention period**: Emails are retained for the period necessary to respond to the inquiry, provide follow-up services and meet legal obligations. When the purpose ends or the retention period expires, data is deleted, its use is stopped or it is de-identified as required by applicable law.

**Region of use**: Taiwan (R.O.C.) and the regions where our email service provider processes data.

**Who uses the data and how**: Our sales and customer service staff reply and contact you by email, phone or similar means. We do not provide your data to third parties except as required by law or with your consent.

You may choose not to provide this information, but we may then be unable to reply to your inquiry or provide the information you need.

## 4. Site search

Site search on this website uses Pagefind. The search index is generated when the website is built and published with it; searching is performed in your browser against that index, and your search terms are not sent to us or to any other search service. For convenience in reloading or sharing, the search terms appear in the page URL (for example `?q=keyword`).

## 5. Hosting and access logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). When you browse this website, your browser requests pages directly from GitHub's servers, and GitHub may log access information such as your IP address to maintain the security and operation of its service. GitHub handles these logs under its privacy statement. Further information is available in the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The fonts, images, videos and search index used on this website are served by this website itself; browsing ordinary pages does not load fonts or tracking scripts from other websites. When you submit the online inquiry form, your browser connects to Google, as described in Section 2.

## 6. External videos and links

Some news articles include YouTube or Facebook videos. This website shows only ordinary links to those platforms; selecting one takes you away from this website, and the platform handles your data under its own privacy policy.

If a video is later approved for display in an embedded player, the player loads only after you select “Load player” (YouTube uses the youtube-nocookie.com domain); once loaded, the platform may collect your data under its own policy.

The address link in the footer opens Google Maps in a new tab and is likewise subject to Google's privacy policy.

## 7. Website content management

The /admin/ path of this website is a tool (Sveltia CMS) for authorized Company personnel to edit website content. It is not intended for visitors and is not indexed by search engines. Personnel sign in with their own GitHub account and personal access token; the token is entered and stored by each person in their own browser, which communicates directly with GitHub. We operate no separate server that receives or stores it. When /admin/ is opened, the browser downloads the Sveltia CMS program from the jsDelivr content delivery network.

## 8. Your rights

With respect to your personal data held by us, you may exercise the following rights under Article 3 of the PDPA:

1. To inquire about or request to review your data.
2. To request a copy.
3. To request supplementation or correction.
4. To request that collection, processing or use be stopped.
5. To request deletion.

Please send your request by email to service@gptt.com.tw. We will handle it within the period required by the PDPA after verifying your identity. Under Article 14 of the PDPA, we may charge a fee covering necessary costs for inquiries, reviews or copies and will explain any such charge in advance.

## 9. Data security

The website uses HTTPS to transmit data and stores inquiries in Google Sheets. Access should be limited to personnel authorized to handle inquiries, with permissions managed according to their work. The receiving script validates fields, checks duplicate submissions and protects against spreadsheet formula injection. Inquiry form content is not written to browser localStorage. The website repository stores no administrator tokens or private Google credentials. Cloud services and email transmission remain subject to the respective providers’ security measures and terms.

## 10. Changes to this Policy

We may revise this Policy in response to changes in law or in this website's services. Revisions are published on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. If the Chinese and English versions differ, the Traditional Chinese version prevails.
