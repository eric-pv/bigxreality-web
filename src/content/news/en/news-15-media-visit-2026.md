Big x Reality (Global Power Technologies) welcomed overseas Chinese-language media representatives for a visit focused on the application of AI and XR technologies in defense, security, and simulation training.

<!-- bxr-image: {"src": "/src/assets/images/news/2026-media-visit-company-briefing.jpg", "alt": "The Big x Reality team introduces the company and its training technologies to the overseas Chinese-language media delegation.", "approved": true} -->

The centerpiece was a hands-on demonstration of the SUT Small Unit Tactical Training System. Wearing VR headsets, visitors entered a simulated defensive position and responded to threats approaching across an approximately 150-degree frontal arc. The sandbag position described in the original social post was part of the virtual scenario, not a physical installation.

<!-- bxr-image: {"src": "/src/assets/images/news/2026-media-visit-vr-training-experience.jpg", "alt": "Media visitors wearing VR headsets experience immersive tactical training with training rifles.", "approved": true} -->

<!-- bxr-image: {"src": "/src/assets/images/news/2026-media-visit-vr-training-stance.jpg", "alt": "Media visitors wearing VR headsets hold training rifles in different stances during a tactical scenario.", "approved": true} -->

<!-- bxr-image: {"src": "/src/assets/images/news/2026-media-visit-instructor-guidance.jpg", "alt": "A team member guides a media visitor using a VR headset and training rifle.", "approved": true} -->

The team also demonstrated its mixed reality training approach, which brings virtual tactical scenarios into real spaces. Additional presentations covered UAV reconnaissance, counter-drone simulation, and unmanned surface vessel monitoring. These demonstrations illustrated how immersive training and unmanned-system technologies can support different mission scenarios.

Big x Reality thanks the visiting media professionals for their interest and conversations. We look forward to sharing Taiwan's AI XR simulation capabilities with a wider international audience.
